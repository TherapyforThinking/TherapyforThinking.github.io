Case Study : Panic Attacks

This kind and confused client was suffering from anxiety that had moved onto full blown panic attacks. It got to the point that they could not work or drive, and social events were traumatic. They had tried all the logical things to help themselves, but instead of resolving things it seemed to make things worse. Very quickly they went from a happy, fully functioning social being, into an introverted, confused person that couldn't find the fun in anything.

What was most surprising?

My opinion has changed drastically! I felt different after my first session, realising that the thoughts/things I was feeling are common and that there are logical explanations for everything my brain/body does. It was comforting to know there is a light at the end of the tunnel.

What were the best things about it?

Learning why I ended up getting into the bad place I was in, finding out more things about myself and seeing that the recovery process is all in my mind. Once you change your mindset you start to see how you can think yourself into these dark places.

What did you find most difficult?

Taking the first step and actually admitting that I needed help. It was a difficult and scary thing to do.

What recommendations would you give to others?

How simple changes in my mindset could have a huge impact on my bodies reactions to various situations. And how quickly you can think yourself into/out of things.
